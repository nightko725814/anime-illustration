# Reference scene profile: Teto as a puppet bride

This is an **example** of a scene profile — a scene-specific recipe kept alongside character profiles. Write one when the user wants to recreate, continue, or adapt a specific scene; use it only for that scene, never as a default for unrelated subjects.

The reference image this profile was written against is not redistributed with this repo. Place your own reference image under `images/` in your project folder and link it here. User-provided changes always take precedence.

- **Subject and crop:** Kasane Teto, with her recognizable red twin-drill curls and red eyes (eyes closed in this scene). Tall 9:16 portrait. Close oblique view from slightly above and in front; head and upper body dominate, with the full white skirt spreading to the lower edges. Place her along a diagonal so hair and shoulders project toward the camera while the skirt recedes.
- **Pose and feeling:** Eyes gently closed, visible lashes, calm and tender with a trace of sorrow. Arms fold across the chest to hold opposite shoulders or upper arms; relaxed hands and slightly inward shoulders make the pose feel vulnerable and lived-in. Suggest a sleeping marionette through stillness, not a rigid pose.
- **Dress and surface detail:** Modest, ornate white bridal gown with layered tulle, satin, lace, fine ruffles, pleats, bows, and a broad weighty skirt. Separate materials with pearl white, cool grey, pale mauve, and faint blue-violet; retain fabric volume and legible folds without blowing out highlights.
- **Marionette threads:** Fine black, dark-red, and silver-grey strings descend from above, corners, and near-camera space. Layer strands with clear directional flow and varied tension; lightly loop a few around wrists, forearms, shoulder, waist, and outer skirt to pull at bows, cuffs, and folds. Keep lines off the face and eyes; no skin indentation or injury. The web should frame and imply control without obscuring the subject.
- **Setting and lens impression:** Soft grey interior corner behind the upper body, with restrained rose and thorn motifs integrated into the walls. Keep the corner legible as the compositional center. Let wall lines bow subtly toward the lens for gentle wide-angle curvature, never fisheye distortion. Use near-camera blurred strings, a few petals, and light particles; keep the subject crisp and the recognizable background softly defocused.
- **Lighting and finish:** Directional soft light from above and in front catches the red hair, closed eyelids, cheeks, and gown folds. Preserve a darker grey corner to separate the silhouette. Concentrate sparse glowing dust in the light beam; vary particle size and blur by distance. Render with fine anime linework and soft semi-painterly shading, with dimensional hair, lace, satin, and fabric tension.
- **Avoid:** open eyes or direct gaze, a frontal eye-level camera, distant small subject, flat white background, rigid or open-armed pose, tangled lines across the face, constricting strings, wounds, violence, revealing or sexualized clothing/pose, blown-out fabric, plastic skin, 3D-render appearance, excessive glow or particles, text, signature, watermark, malformed hands, or extra limbs.
