Rich, carefully composed collage poster background for an anime character illustration. Kasane Teto, a girl with crimson spiral drill twin-tails, red eyes, dark headphones and a charcoal uniform with a red tie, is already drawn slightly right of center, hugging a long baguette in a brown kraft-paper bag, softly lit from the front. Keep her exactly as she is; her body continues past the bottom edge of the poster and nothing cuts across her. Design only the collage around her.

Build a layered collage from well-made, complete components, all drawn in the same clean anime illustration style with crisp edges, flat print colors and clear dark outlines: a large white paper semicircle cut-out behind her shoulder, a panel of coarse red halftone dots on blush-pink paper, a sheet of faint graph paper, a long black film-negative strip with empty frames running diagonally across the upper right, two long sheet-music strips, a cassette tape with a loose loop of magnetic tape, two blank ticket stubs, two polaroid photos showing only soft out-of-focus crimson shapes, several straight transparent tape strips, one electric-cyan paper block, one acid-yellow paper block, a few black cut-paper silhouettes, thin crimson technical lines and two clean ink loops. Concentrate the richest cluster around the baguette bag at the lower left and a second cluster around her right twin-tail at the upper right. Keep a wide horizontal band across the left side, from about one fifth to one half of the height and from the left edge to her hair, as a calm, empty, flat crimson field for a large title that will be added later, and keep a blank off-white strip at the right edge at mid-height for a small label.

Palette: crimson and scarlet dominant, warm off-white, blush pink, matte black, small touches of electric cyan, acid yellow and mint. Tactile, high-end editorial collage poster with clear hierarchy: the character first, then the two collage clusters, then the flat color fields.
