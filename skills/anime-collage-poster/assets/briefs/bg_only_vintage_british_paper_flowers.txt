Vintage British poster collage with handmade paper flower art on the left side of an anime poster. Kasane Teto, a girl with crimson spiral drill twin-tails, red eyes, dark headphones and a charcoal uniform with a red tie, hugging a long baguette in a brown kraft-paper bag, is already drawn right of center. Keep her and everything to her right exactly as they are; design only the left part of the poster.

Fill the left side with layered, torn fragments of vintage British posters: faded lithograph prints with flat muted colors and visible paper grain, showing a red double-decker bus, a red telephone box, a Big Ben silhouette against a dusk sky, a section of a Union Jack, an ornamental royal crest shape, vintage postage stamps with perforated edges and a round postmark, and a strip of old letterpress border ornament. Mix them with torn blush-pink paper, warm off-white paper, black paper, graph paper and coarse red halftone dots. Layer the fragments at different angles with rough torn edges, curled corners, soft paper shadows and transparent tape, breaking apart toward the left and top edges so the left side feels strongly fragmented.

Add handmade paper flower art: a lush cluster of crepe-paper roses, folded origami flowers and paper leaves in crimson, blush pink, cream and white, rising from the lower left corner up along the left edge, with crisp folded petals and visible paper texture.

Break up the flat red: crimson stays present but mixes with cream, faded navy, mustard, blush pink and black, plus small accents of electric cyan and acid yellow. Keep the upper left a little calmer so a title can be placed over it later.

Every fragment and flower is clean, complete and drawn in the same illustration style. The poster fragments are pictures only: no text, no letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces, no extra copies of the character. No confetti dust, no paint splatter, no noise.
